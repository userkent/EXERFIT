Weather forecast

ExerFit uses OpenWeather for live conditions and its five-day forecast. Add your own API key to enable weather panels, rain advice, and route weather.

The key is saved in this browser's local app data and sent to OpenWeather when weather is requested. Do not use a private production key in a public deployment without a server-side proxy.
