Everything in one place

Built around the way you move.

ExerFit brings planning, activity records, and personal goals into one simple dashboard. Outdoor routes and indoor sessions both belong here.

01

Track your activity

Record outdoor GPS sessions, indoor cardio, strength sets, repetitions, and mind-and-body activities.

02

Plan with context

Explore an OpenStreetMap, plan a route, and check available local weather before heading out.

03

See your progress

Bring your activity history, personal goals, streaks, and weekly patterns together.

04

Indoor and outdoor

Switch between walking, running, cycling, gym training, bodyweight workouts, and more.

05

Weather-aware planning

Use current conditions and the available five-day, three-hour forecast to plan outdoor sessions.

06

Your data, your account

Each local demo account keeps its own profile, activities, goals, preferences, and saved routes.
