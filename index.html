ABOUT EXERFIT

One place for the way you move.

ExerFit brings outdoor activity tracking, route exploration, and indoor workout planning into one simple fitness workspace.

Our purpose

Make it easier to plan movement, record progress, and understand your activity in a single place. Whether you are taking a short walk or building a weekly workout routine, ExerFit helps you keep the details organized.

Explore with context

Use an interactive map to search destinations, view supported route options, and save route geometry for another day. GPS-based activity tracks remain separate from planned routes.

Track your movement

Record walking, running, cycling, or hiking with your device's location permission. Review distance and duration after an activity is completed.

Plan indoor training

Organize exercises, sets, repetitions, rest periods, and workout notes for gym or home sessions. Mark routines complete and keep a record of them.

BUILT WITH OPEN TECHNOLOGY

Designed to be understandable and adaptable.

ExerFit is a learning prototype built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Leaflet, and open map data. Map, routing, geocoding, GPS, and weather features depend on browser permissions and the availability and terms of their connected services.
