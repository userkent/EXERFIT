Move with purpose.

One connected space for the way you move.

ExerFit is an activity-tracking website built to bring everyday movement into one clear experience. Whether you walk outdoors, train indoors, work toward a goal, or look back at your activity history, the tools are designed to stay together and easy to follow.

How this demo works

Built to be clear about your data.

ExerFit is a front-end student demonstration. Account details, preferences, goals, and activity records are stored locally in the browser used for the demo; they are not synced to a secure application server.
